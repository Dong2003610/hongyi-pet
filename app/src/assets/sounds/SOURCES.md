# Sound asset sources

## `eat.wav`

- Title: **Potato Chip Crunch**
- Creator: **TheRichardGamer**
- Source: <https://freesound.org/people/TheRichardGamer/sounds/191653/>
- License: [Creative Commons Zero 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (CC0)
- Source description: "A recording I made while I was eating a pringles potato chip."

The Freesound high-quality preview was converted from MP3 to mono, 22050 Hz,
16-bit PCM WAV for Electron playback. Its level was reduced by 1 dB and very
short fades were added at both ends to prevent playback clicks. No synthetic
sound was mixed into the recording.
